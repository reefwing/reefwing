# About Me

I’m an embedded systems engineer, author and founder of Reefwing Software, based in Sydney, Australia. I build software and hardware for embedded AI, IoT, robotics and drone flight control systems.
My work spans more than 30 years of embedded development, including senior roles at Serco Australia, Honeywell and Tyco. I enjoy turning engineering ideas into working devices—and sharing the code, designs and lessons along the way.

## Embedded AI: Intelligence at the Deep Edge
My book, published by No Starch Press, explores how to build, deploy and test artificial intelligence on resource-constrained devices. It includes more than 25 hands-on projects covering sensors, machine learning, audio processing and embedded inference, with downloadable code and hardware resources.
- 📘 [Get the book from No Starch Press](https://nostarch.com/embedded-ai)
- 💻 [Browse the source code and resources](https://github.com/Reefwing-Software/Embedded-AI)

## What you’ll find here
This account holds my personal projects and contributions, including Magpie drone hardware. Most of my libraries, embedded AI examples and shared development tools live in the Reefwing Software organisation.

## My interests include:
- Embedded AI and machine learning on microcontrollers.
- Sensor fusion, robotics and flight control.
- PCB design and the firmware.
- Practical engineering education and open-source development.
  
I also teach STEM subjects, including electronics, programming, robotics and drones. My studies span electrical engineering, computing science, physics, business and wine science—because there is always something else worth learning.
